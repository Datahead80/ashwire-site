The burn cycle

How we build

Every product goes through the same loop. Ideas that don't survive it go back to ash, and the next one starts from what we learned.

  1. 01

    Spark

    Find a real problem or a missing kind of fun, and test it with people before writing code.

  2. 02

    Wire

    Build the smallest version with the AI doing the hard part, and cut everything else.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Put it in front of real users in weeks, then watch what they actually do.

  4. 04

    Rise

    Double down on what works. Burn down what doesn't, and carry the lessons forward.
