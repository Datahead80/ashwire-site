How Cinderbox protects you

Use whatever AI you like. You decide what it gets to know about you.

Last updated October 27, 2026 · Ashwire Labs

This page says what Cinderbox does today and where it stops. It is written to be checked, so it names the limits as plainly as the strengths. The details of what we collect are in the privacy policy.

What it does

  • Runs on your phone. The app has no internet permission. There is no Cinderbox server holding your facts, and no account.
  • Locks your facts. They are kept in an encrypted database on the phone. The key sits in Android's secure key storage, and the app opens with your fingerprint or screen lock and locks itself shortly after you leave.
  • Shows you everything first. Before anything leaves, you see the exact text. Nothing goes unless you tap send.
  • Gives you three sharing levels. Each fact is Never, Ask or Open. Never facts are kept out of what the app prepares and need a fresh fingerprint to view.
  • Refuses passwords. Cinderbox blocks passwords, keys and card numbers on the way in and again on the way out.
  • Can hide names. Names you saved under People can be swapped for placeholders in the text you send.
  • Keeps a plain record. The log lists which AI app got which fact labels and when. It never stores the values.
  • Encrypts backups. A backup file is encrypted with an eight-word recovery phrase that only you hold.
  • Keeps any on-phone AI model on a short leash. It is a model file you choose, it runs on the phone, it reads only what the locker lets it read, and it is unloaded when the app locks.

What it does not do

  • It cannot control the AI app you send to. After you tap send, that app has the text. Send only what you are happy for it to have.
  • Name hiding is limited. It only covers names you saved under People. A name that appears only in pasted text, an email address or a phone number is not hidden for you today, so read the preview.
  • It is not anonymous. Dates, places and other details in what you send can still point to you.
  • The clipboard is shared. Cinderbox clears what it copied after 30 seconds, but your keyboard's clipboard history may have kept it.
  • A lost phone and a lost recovery phrase are not recoverable by us. We hold no copy of your facts or your phrase.
  • It is not finished. Cinderbox is in development, for Android first. It has not had an independent security test yet, and we will not call it proven until it has.

How you can check

  • Look at the app's permission list in Android settings. The internet is not on it.
  • Read the preview before every send. What you see is what goes.
  • Open the log to see what each AI app received.
