Cinderbox · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Use whatever AI you like. You decide what it gets to know about you.

Last updated October 27, 2026 · Ashwire Labs

The short version. The Cinderbox app does not collect your data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet itself. Text leaves only when you hand it to another app, after you see it. There is no account, no analytics and no ads.

What the app keeps on your phone

Everything you put in Cinderbox stays on your phone:

  • The facts you save, and the notes you add to them.
  • A log of what you share from the Share screen: which AI app, which fact labels, how many, and when. The log never holds the values themselves. Copying a single item from its own page is not logged.
  • Your settings.
  • If you choose to set one up, an AI model file (about 1 to 3 GB) that you download and pick. It runs only on the phone. Today it runs a speed check on made-up facts and can tidy a note you write. It never sees items marked Never.

The facts are stored in an encrypted database. Its key is held in Android's secure key storage, and the app asks for your fingerprint or phone screen lock to open. It locks itself after a delay you choose (right away, 1 minute by default, or 5 minutes), hides its screens from screenshots and the recent-apps view, and turns off Android's cloud backup for its data.

What leaves your phone, and only when you say so

Cinderbox shows you the exact text before anything leaves. When you tap Open in an AI app (such as ChatGPT or Claude), Cinderbox hands that exact text to the app you picked, ready to send, and that app has it from that moment. If you choose Copy instead, the text goes to the clipboard. Cinderbox clears it after 30 seconds, or as soon as you return to Cinderbox, but your keyboard's own clipboard history may keep a copy.

Once text is in another AI app, that app's own privacy policy applies. Cinderbox does not control what it does with it.

If you export a backup, you get a file encrypted with an eight-word recovery phrase. You choose where the file goes. Cinderbox never uploads it.

What we receive

Nothing from the app. We get something only if you email us. Then we see your email address and what you wrote. The feedback button prefills your email with the app version and nothing from your locker. We use your message to reply and to fix problems, and we do not sell or share it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Fingerprint or screen lock, to open the app and to show items you marked Never.
  • Seeing whether five AI apps are installed (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Meta AI and Grok), so it can show you a button for each one you have.
  • Text shared into the app, when you use Android's Share and pick Cinderbox.

Android also lists a few small system permissions: fingerprint, vibration, and detecting screen capture. On Android 13 and older, a library the app uses adds a photo-read permission. Cinderbox does not read your photos today.

It does not ask for the internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or access to your files. It blocks the internet, storage and screen-overlay permissions on purpose.

This website

ashwire.ai is static pages. It has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no forms. The one outside request is the page fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details under Google's own policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement. To join the waitlist you send us an email. We keep your address and the phone model you tell us only to invite you to the beta, and we never sell or share them. We keep emails until you ask us to delete them, or for 12 months after the beta ends.

Deleting your data

In Settings, Delete everything erases your items, notes, saved tasks, names and log. Your backup words stay so you can restore a backup, and you can delete the on-phone model in Settings. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it holds on the phone. We hold nothing about you to delete, other than any emails you have sent us, which you can ask us to remove.

Children

Cinderbox is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Links

Links in the app open your browser or email app. The model download page is on Hugging Face, which sees your IP address when you open it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and say what changed on the Cinderbox page.
