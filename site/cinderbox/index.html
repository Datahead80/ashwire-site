Four parts, one app

How it works

Cinderbox puts a private locker, your own agent and a smart prompt library in one app. It runs on your phone, so there is no Cinderbox server holding your data.

01 · LOCKER

Your facts, locked down

Store the details AI keeps asking for: your diet, your car, your kids' ages, your work history. Encrypted on your phone and opened with your fingerprint.

02 · AGENT

An AI you own

An open-weight model runs right on your phone and reads your locker, even in airplane mode. No account with OpenAI, Google or Meta needed.

03 · PROMPTS

Prompts that fill themselves

Save a prompt once with blanks like {diet} or {car}. Cinderbox fills them from your locker, so you stop retyping your life.

04 · CONTEXT PACK

Approve what leaves

When you want a bigger cloud AI, Cinderbox builds a pack of only the facts the task needs. You see the exact list and untick anything before it leaves.

You set the rules

Three sharing levels

Every fact in your locker gets one of three levels, and you can change it any time.

  • open

    Your agent can use it freely, and it can go in a context pack.

  • ask

    Cinderbox asks you first, every time it wants to use it.

  • never

    Locked away. Your agent never sees it and it never leaves your phone.

Planned after launch

See, decide, prove

Cinderbox is built to keep your data from leaking out. Once the locker ships, we plan to add tools that show where your data goes and let you decide. These are goals, not features you can use today.

  • See

    Which trackers are in your apps, and what left your phone through Cinderbox.

  • Decide

    For each flow, you choose: block it, use an alias, or share real data after you approve it.

  • Prove

    A monthly Data Receipt you can read, keep and show. Nothing is sent for you.
