Core values

What we stand for

AI should make life easier without costing people their privacy, their money or what makes them who they are. These five principles shape every product we build and every action our agents take.

  1. 01

    Human first

    The app works for you, not the other way round. AI suggests; you decide. Nothing important happens without your say.

  2. 02

    Your data stays yours

    Private by default. On your device wherever we can, never sold, and never shared without your clear approval.

  3. 03

    Does what it says

    No hype. If we advertise a feature, it works, and we tell you plainly what the AI can't do.

  4. 04

    Lean by design

    Small, efficient models and fair prices. We use AI only where it earns its place, so it costs you less.

  5. 05

    Time back, not attention taken

    We measure success by the time we give you back, not the time you spend in our apps. No engagement traps.

The mission test

Does it stay true to the mission?

  1. Does it put the person first and leave them in control?
  2. Does it protect their data?
  3. Does it do exactly what we say it does?
  4. Is it the leanest way to get the job done?
  5. Does it give people time back?

Every feature, release and agent action has to pass all five. If it doesn't, we don't ship it.

Our process

How we build

Every product goes through the same four steps. The phoenix is our logo because of the last one: when something doesn't work, we take what we learned and build it better.

  1. 01

    Spark

    Find a real problem or a missing kind of fun, and test it with people before writing code.

  2. 02

    Wire

    Build the smallest version with the AI doing the hard part, and cut everything else.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Put it in front of real users in weeks, then watch what they actually do.

  4. 04

    Rise

    Do more of what works. Drop what doesn't, and build the next version better.

Our mission
